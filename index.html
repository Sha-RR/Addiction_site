What We Offer

Comprehensive
Treatment Services

From first evaluation to long-term recovery maintenance — we cover the full continuum of addiction care.

01
💊

Medication-Assisted Treatment

Buprenorphine/naloxone, naltrexone, and methadone programs for opioid and alcohol use disorders under close physician supervision and ongoing monitoring.

Learn More →
02
🧠

Dual Diagnosis Treatment

Co-occurring depression, anxiety, PTSD, and bipolar disorders treated alongside addiction by our integrated behavioral health and medical teams.

Learn More →
03
🤝

Intensive Outpatient (IOP)

Structured group therapy, individual counseling, and medical check-ins — comprehensive treatment without requiring inpatient admission.

Learn More →
04
🔬

Medical Detoxification

Physician-supervised withdrawal management for alcohol, opioids, benzodiazepines, and stimulants — minimizing discomfort and medical risk safely.

Learn More →
05

Telehealth Services

Secure video appointments for medication management, therapy, and follow-up consultations — accessible from anywhere across California.

Alumni groups, ongoing medication management, wellness coaching, and community referrals to build and sustain long-term sobriety.
