Who We Are

A New Standard in
Addiction Medicine

Clarity Health was founded on the belief that addiction is a complex, chronic medical condition — not a moral failing. Our multidisciplinary team combines cutting-edge medical care with behavioral health expertise and compassionate support.

We serve patients across the Inland Empire with individualized treatment plans, integrating medication-assisted treatment (MAT), psychotherapy, and long-term wellness strategies to build sustainable recovery.

Evidence-Based

All protocols follow SAMHSA and ASAM clinical guidelines.

Whole-Person Care

Mental, physical, and social wellbeing addressed together.

Confidential

HIPAA-compliant, stigma-free environment at every visit.

Accessible

Most major insurance plans accepted; telehealth available.

What We Offer

Comprehensive Treatment Services

From initial evaluation to long-term recovery support, our services span the full continuum of addiction care.

💊

Medication-Assisted Treatment

Buprenorphine/naloxone (Suboxone), naltrexone (Vivitrol), and methadone programs for opioid and alcohol use disorders under close physician supervision.

MAT Program
🧠

Dual Diagnosis Treatment

Co-occurring mental health conditions including depression, anxiety, PTSD, and bipolar disorder treated alongside addiction by integrated care teams.

Integrated Care
🤝

Outpatient Programs (IOP/OP)

Intensive and standard outpatient programs offering structured group therapy, individual counseling, and medical check-ins without inpatient admission.

Flexible Scheduling
🔬

Medical Detoxification

Physician-supervised withdrawal management for alcohol, opioids, benzodiazepines, and stimulants — safely reducing discomfort and medical risk.

Safe Detox
💻

Telehealth Services

Secure video appointments for medication management, therapy, and follow-up consultations — accessible from home throughout California.

CA Statewide
🌱

Aftercare & Relapse Prevention

Alumni support groups, ongoing medication management, wellness coaching, and community referrals to maintain long-term sobriety.

Long-Term Support

Meet Your Care Team

👨‍⚕️
Dr. Marcus J. Reyes, MD
Medical Director · Board-Certified Addiction Medicine
Dr. Reyes has over 18 years of experience in addiction medicine and internal medicine. A graduate of UCLA School of Medicine, he completed his addiction medicine fellowship at UC San Francisco and is an active member of ASAM and CSAM.
Opioid Use Disorder Alcohol Dependence MAT
👩‍⚕️
Dr. Priya Anand, MD, MPH
Attending Physician · Psychiatry & Addiction Medicine
Dr. Anand is dual board-certified in psychiatry and addiction medicine. Trained at Stanford and Johns Hopkins, she specializes in co-occurring disorders and brings a trauma-informed lens to every patient relationship.
Dual Diagnosis Trauma-Informed Mood Disorders
👨‍⚕️
Dr. Thomas W. Chen, DO
Associate Physician · Family & Addiction Medicine
Dr. Chen brings a whole-person, osteopathic approach to addiction medicine. A graduate of Western University of Health Sciences, he focuses on long-term recovery maintenance and patient education in the Inland Empire community.
Stimulant Disorders Wellness Telehealth
👩‍⚕️
Dr. Renée L. Fontaine, MD
Addiction Medicine Fellow · Loma Linda University
Dr. Fontaine completed her residency in internal medicine at Loma Linda University Medical Center and is currently completing her addiction medicine fellowship. She has a special interest in adolescent substance use and prevention.
Adolescents Prevention Buprenorphine
👨‍⚕️
Dr. Samuel O. Adeyemi, PhD, LMFT
Director of Behavioral Health
Dr. Adeyemi leads our behavioral health division with a doctorate in clinical psychology and licensure as a marriage and family therapist. He oversees group therapy, CBT, and motivational interviewing programs across all levels of care.
CBT Motivational Interviewing Group Therapy
👩‍⚕️
Maria G. Torres, PMHNP-BC
Psychiatric Nurse Practitioner
Maria is a board-certified psychiatric mental health nurse practitioner with a decade of experience in addiction and mental health settings. She manages medication protocols and patient education for our telehealth and outpatient programs.
Medication Mgmt Telehealth Patient Education
